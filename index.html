AnnotateUp for Mac

AnnotateUp is built for fast, structured image annotation. Whether you’re labeling equipment, marking objects, documenting inspections, or adding quick reference notes, it gives you a simple workflow designed for clarity and speed — no formatting, no styling decisions, no friction.

Fast, Direct Markups

Draw directly on your images with tools built for quick, accurate labeling. Just mark what matters and move on.

Automatic Numbering

Each new mark is numbered for you, keeping annotations organized without any manual setup. Turn numbering off anytime for a cleaner layout.

Directional Arrows

Automatically adds arrows based on the direction you draw — ideal for marking flow direction and paths. Toggle on or off as needed.

Attached Notes

Add notes linked to each numbered label. They appear directly on the image, connecting details to the exact point they refer to.

Simple Image Input

Choose from your photo library, import from Files, or paste directly from clipboard. Seamless integration for any workflow.
